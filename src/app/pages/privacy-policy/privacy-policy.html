2. Hosting and server logs

This website and the automation server that generates the recipes run on servers of an external hosting provider in Germany. When you open the website, the server automatically stores your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, your browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website securely and to detect errors and attacks (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The log files are deleted after seven days at the latest, unless they are needed to clarify a security incident.

3. Recipe generation with Google Gemini

When you request recipes, your ingredients and preferences (servings, cooks, cooking time, cuisine and diet) are sent to my automation server. It passes them on to Google Gemini, a language model service of Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (USA), which writes the recipes. No name, email address or IP address is passed on to Google. The free version of the Gemini API is used: Google may store the content it receives and use it to improve its services, and human reviewers may read it. Please only enter ingredients and no personal information. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, my interest in offering the recipe generator.

To keep the costs of the free service under control, each IP address may request recipes three times a day. The automation server keeps a counter per IP address for the current day only and resets all counters at midnight (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

4. Cookbook and likes

The generated recipes are published in the shared cookbook without any reference to you. They and the number of likes are stored in a database of Supabase Inc. (USA), which processes the data on my behalf under a data processing agreement. Supabase is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. To answer requests, Supabase also processes your IP address for a short time (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

5. Storage in your browser

Your browser keeps the following entries on your device; they are not sent to the server:

  • your entries in the generator, until you close the tab (session storage),
  • the recipes you liked, so you can like each recipe only once,
  • the cooking steps you ticked off,
  • how many recipe requests you have left today.

These entries are strictly necessary for the functions you use (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete them at any time in your browser settings. The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising; the fonts are stored on this server.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • have your data erased or its processing restricted (Art. 17 and 18 GDPR),
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

Just write to the email address above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner.
